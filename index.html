Overview

Built to keep up with whatever moves.

Sony built the 6100-series around one problem: keeping focus on a subject that won't sit still. This kit pairs that AI-driven tracking with two lenses — a compact power-zoom for everyday and vlog work, and a 55-210mm reach lens for subjects further out — so you're not shopping for glass separately on day one.

Buying perspective

What works — and what to think about.

Why consider it

  • Two lenses in one box covers wide-to-tele without separate purchases
  • Real-time Eye AF makes focus feel nearly automatic for people and pets
  • 0.02s AF and 11fps burst handle fast, unpredictable subjects
  • Tiltable screen suits solo vlogging without extra rigging
  • Compact, light body for all-day or travel carry

Before you buy

  • Variable-aperture kit lenses are slower in low light than fixed-aperture glass
  • Confirm you're buying the twin-lens kit and not a body-only or single-lens listing
  • 4K video specifics (crop, bit-rate) are worth checking against your editing needs
  • SELP1650 is a power-zoom — zoom speed differs from a manual ring
  • Check today's price, seller and warranty terms on the listing
